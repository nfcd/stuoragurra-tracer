      How to use

      Paper's interpretation loads the scarp traces and profile review used in the paper. You can also map your own scarps.

      Map

      • 1 elevation, 2 hillshade, 3 texture shading, 4 scarp index. F stretches the elevation colours to the view.

      Tracing

      • T trace: click along a scarp; Enter or double-click finishes.
      • V select a trace, then M move, I insert or D delete points. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes.
      • H hides the selected trace, Shift+H shows all.

      Profiles

      • P draws a profile between two points.
      • R reviews profiles every 100 m along the traces: Y accept, N reject, U unsure, ← / → previous / next.

      Saving

      • Work is saved in this browser. Export GeoJSON and Export review save it as files; New clears everything.
